# How to Play Pitch Quiz on CHOMPI

Listen to the sound, then press the button for the pitch you think you heard. The game will tell you if you got it right.

3 attemptes per round (progress visible on black keys)

10 random rounds per level

10 levels with increasing difficulty

## Buttons

- **Play/Pause**: Start the game. (hold to return to idle mode)
- **LOOP**: replay the sound for the question during game
- **SHIFT + Black Keys 1-10**: select level 1-10, will automatically start.
- **SHIFT + LOOP**: Trigger Reboot prompt, reboot with another SHIFT press
- **Volume Encoder**: Set volume level

## Levels

Each level makes the quiz a little harder. You may need to tell apart pitches that sound more alike, so listen closely! Can you get ten out of ten?

If you finish level 10 with no wrong answers, congratulations, you saved the princess, now play again!
